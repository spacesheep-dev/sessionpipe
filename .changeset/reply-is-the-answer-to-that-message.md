The reply to a message delivered in place (at a session's Stop, to its waiter, into its inbox) is now the answer to that message, or nothing. Three things went wrong on one day: a message handed over at a Stop came back with the sentence the session had written before the message arrived (Claude Code writes its transcript a moment after the fact, so the turn's last words landed after its Stop hook had asked); a message to a session that also had a copy was read from the copy's transcript, so its ack waited 215 seconds and carried a later message's answer; and another such message was acked 30 minutes late with no reply at all. The message now names itself in its frame, by the last twelve characters of its id (`…signed on the sender's device; message XXXXXXXXXXXX):`, and for an ask `…with sessionpipe ask, message XXXXXXXXXXXX.`), the reply is what the session wrote after the rows its transcript records for that message and before anything else begins (the next message, a prompt the person types, a turn a background task starts), it is read from the transcript of the session the message was handed to, and the final ack goes out when the session's own hooks say that turn ended. Where no answer can be put down to the message the ack is `delivered` without a `reply`, and no longer carries a file path in `detail` when the transcript can't be read.
