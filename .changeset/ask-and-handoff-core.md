Control: a handoff's `reply_to`, and the events of an ask (CONTROL.md §3.1, §6 *Handoff*, §13; PROTOCOL.md §3, §4). A `prompt` or a `start` MAY carry `reply_to`, `<harness>:<id>` of a session on the same machine that gets the answer as a message once the command's turn ends. `sessionpipe-core/control` accepts it on those two kinds only, as a session ref by the adapter's rule and never the command's own session, and refuses the rest `bad_fields`; the command's acks are unchanged. Two event types on the asking session, `delegation.started` and `delegation.ended` (tier 0; `text` at tier 1, `reply` at tier 2), and one session field, `delegated_by` (from `SESSIONPIPE_ASKED_BY`, read like `launched_by`), say which session asked which and what came back. Schemas: `control-command` gains `reply_to`, `control-hello` and `control-pair` gain `ask` and `handoff`, `event` gains `session.delegated_by`, `event-data` the two types. Five new shared vectors (`137`–`141`).
