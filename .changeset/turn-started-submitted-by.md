`turn.started` can now say who started the turn: two optional tier-0 fields, `submitted_by` (the harness's own word for who submitted the prompt, forwarded verbatim and never mapped onto a vocabulary of the spec — a different vocabulary from `session.started.source`) and `injected` (`true` when the prompt text is a block the harness wrote rather than words a person typed). Claude Code fills `submitted_by` from `UserPromptSubmit.source` when a payload carries that key — its hook schema documents `user` · `sdk` · `system` · `loop_wakeup` · `schedule_wakeup` · `poll_event` and says a payload may omit it while the field rolls out, and no build through 2.1.304 sends it — and sets `injected` from a closed list of its own prompt wrappers (`<task-notification>`, `<cross-session-message …>`, `<system-reminder>`, `<local-command-stdout>`, `<command-…>`), so a task notification, a peer message or a slash-command expansion is distinguishable from a person typing. `injected: true` says only that the prompt is not the person's words, never that no person caused the turn: a Talk or `sessionpipe wait` delivery arrives inside a `<task-notification>`. Both fields are absent when the harness does not say, and an absent field is unknown, never "the person". `sessionpipe tail` prints both. Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and Devin leave both absent.
