## You may

- Use "sessionpipe" to refer to this project, its protocol and its software.
- Say that your product **"supports sessionpipe"**, **"speaks the sessionpipe
  protocol"** or **"is a sessionpipe receiver"**, truthfully.
- Link to sessionpipe.org and use the name in documentation, talks and articles.
- Publish a conformance badge on a receiver that passes the suite.

## Please do not

- Use "sessionpipe" as, or as part of, your own product's name, company name,
  domain name, npm package name or social handle (`sessionpipe-*` is fine for an
  adapter or plugin that works *with* sessionpipe, provided the name makes that clear).

  affiliated with your product or organisation.
- Modify the logo.
- Ship a fork under the sessionpipe name. Forks are welcome under the license;
